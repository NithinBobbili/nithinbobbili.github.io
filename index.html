About

Six years of turning raw feeds into decisions people trust.

I'm a Senior Data Engineer with 6+ years architecting and delivering large-scale data platforms across banking, insurance, pharmaceutical, and fintech domains — industries where a broken pipeline isn't just annoying, it's a compliance problem. I hold a Master's in Computer Science from the University of Dayton (2023).

My focus is cloud-native lakehouse design, real-time streaming, and medallion-based ETL/ELT on AWS and Azure — with Python, Spark, Kafka, Snowflake, Delta Lake, and Terraform as the daily toolkit. I've led mission-critical pipelines for AUM reporting, credit risk analytics, fraud detection, and clinical data governance, working within PCI-DSS, GDPR, FCRA, HIPAA, and GxP compliance frameworks.

Right now I'm a Senior Data Engineer at Nationwide, where I own month-end financial reporting pipelines, led a lakehouse migration to Databricks that cut processing time by 40%, and built a real-time fraud detection pipeline on Event Hub and Kafka. Before that: Comcast (streaming platforms at telecom scale), Experian (credit risk analytics), and Novartis (clinical data pipelines).

I'm looking for full-time work where I can keep doing exactly this: solving the unglamorous, high-stakes problem of getting the right data to the right place, fast, correctly, and compliantly.
